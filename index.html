On-phone AI reader

A small AI model that runs inside this browser. It reads reviews and pulls out what matters for celiac safety, quoting the exact sentence each time. Nothing is sent anywhere. The first load downloads the model once; after that it's stored on the phone.

About the grades

Grocery grades come from Open Food Facts (a free, crowd-built product database) plus gluten rules: wheat, barley, rye, malt, uncertified oats, and the other grains that hide on labels. US law requires wheat to be named but not barley or rye, so the rules look for those by name. Database entries can be out of date. When it matters, check the package.

Restaurant findings are only as good as the reviews and menus you add. No online source can rule out cross-contact in a kitchen, so call ahead.
